Tim Swanson, an assistant special agent in charge at FBI Pittsburgh, and Bianca Pearson, a supervisory senior resident agent who oversees FBI Charlotte’s satellite offices in Asheville and Hickory, North Carolina, recently became the FBI National Academy program's first-ever special-agent graduates.
